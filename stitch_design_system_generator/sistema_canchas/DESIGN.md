## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a clean, high-contrast, and energetic visual identity tailored for sports facility management. The design narrative balances rugged utility with modern precision, evoking reliability, speed, and agility. 

The aesthetic style is rooted in a crisp, functional **Corporate / Modern** framework infused with high-contrast energetic cues. It avoids gratuitous decoration in favor of structural clarity, robust information density, and instant legibility across all form factors.

### Core Pillars
- **Uncompromising Contrast:** High-accessibility standards dictate every pairing, ensuring effortless scanning under varying environmental conditions (from sunlit outdoor mobile screens to dimly lit indoor administrative desks).
- **Purposeful Energy:** The vibrant "Energy Green" primary color drives calls to action, while neutral charcoal tones ground the interface, preventing visual fatigue during extended management sessions.
- **Contextual Semantics:** Color is never purely decorative; it communicates state, availability, and sport-specific categorization with strict consistency.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an energetic primary green scale, green-tinted charcoal neutrals, restricted accent tones, and strict semantic pairings.

### Primary & Accent
- **Verde Energía (`#00B86B`):** The definitive brand marker and primary interactive color. *Rule:* Always paired with charcoal text (`#0D1211`) rather than white to guarantee optimal visual contrast.
- **Lime (`#C8EE4B`):** Reserved exclusively for dark mode surfaces (`neutral-950` / `900`) such as inverted logos, footers, and empty state banners.

### Neutrals (Charcoal Scale)
The neutral palette utilizes subtle green undertones (`#0D1211` to `#FFFFFF`) to maintain visual harmony with the primary brand color across both light and dark environments.

### Semantics & Sport Colors
- **Semantic States:** Dedicated tones for success (reusing brand green), warnings (amber `#FEF4E2`), errors (`#FDECEC`), and informational notices.
- **Sport-Specific Identifiers:** Restricted strictly to micro-elements (chips and icons) to differentiate activities (Fútbol, Vóley, Básquet, Tenis, Padel) without cluttering the interface.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative yet accessible hierarchy. **Sora** drives headings and display roles with geometric rhythm (never dropping below weight 600), while **Inter** delivers clean, highly legible body copy and interface labels.

### Tabular Data & Metrics
For hours, prices, and counters, always enable tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) using monospace variants where necessary to maintain vertical alignment in schedules and financial summaries (e.g., `18:00 – 19:00`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a responsive **fluid grid** system anchored to a strict 4px spacing rhythm. 

### Grid Specifications
- **Desktop ($\ge$ 1024px):** 12-column grid with 32px gutters and 32px outer canvas margins.
- **Tablet ($\ge$ 768px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 16px outer margins.

### Rhythm & Containers
Section spacing scales dynamically between 48px and 64px. Maintain strict container constraints (640px / 880px / 1120px / 1280px) and adhere to the maximum prose width (`--container-prose` optimized for 68 characters per line) to ensure comfortable readability.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is rendered using specialized green-tinted shadows (`rgb(13 18 17 / …)`) rather than pure black, reinforcing the organic energy of the brand. In dark mode surfaces, shadow opacities are doubled to maintain clear separation.

### Elevation Tiers
- **`xs`**: Subtle separation for badges, inline tags, and active chips.
- **`sm`**: Resting cards and focused input states.
- **`md`**: Floating dropdown menus and popovers.
- **`lg`**: Hover card states featuring a clean upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) and sidebars.
- **`xl`**: Prominent overlays, modals, and drawers, paired with a dedicated backdrop overlay variable (`--bg-overlay`).

## Shapes

The shape language utilizes a friendly yet structured **Rounded** geometry (`roundedness` level 2), balancing professional administrative clarity with approachable consumer touchpoints.

### Border Radii Hierarchy
- **`xs` (6px):** Small tags, avatars, micro-indicators.
- **`sm` (8px):** Inputs, compact buttons, schedule cells, form fields, toasts, inline alerts.
- **`md` (12px):** Standard buttons, dropdown containers, popovers.
- **`lg` (16px):** Content cards, panels, modal containers.
- **`xl` (20px) / `2xl` (28px):** Featured promotional cards, large empty states, bottom mobile drawers.
- **`full` (999px):** Chips, pill badges, switches, and icon buttons.

Borders consistently apply a 1-pixel width using `border-default` (`neutral-200`) or `border-subtle` (`neutral-100`).

## Components

Components follow a strict *"One screen, one primary action"* rule, prioritizing high information density and immediate clarity.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled with `primary-500` (`#00B86B`) and paired strictly with charcoal text (`#0D1211`). Hover states transition smoothly to `primary-600`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Utilize neutral surface fills (`neutral-50` / `neutral-100`) with high-contrast text labels.
- **Padding:** Medium buttons maintain 12px vertical and 20px horizontal padding.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Default field backgrounds use `neutral-0` with `neutral-200` borders, transitioning to primary green focus rings with `sm` border radii (8px). 
- Form fields maintain consistent 20px vertical gaps. Field labels use the `label` typography style (12px, semibold, non-uppercase).

### Cards & Containers
- Standard cards feature 20px internal padding, `lg` border radii (16px), and resting `sm` shadows. 
- Hover states on interactive cards trigger the `lg` shadow elevation combined with a subtle `translateY(-2px)` transition.

### Chips & Badges
- Used for sport categories, filters, and status markers. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), utilizing soft tinted backgrounds (`primary-50`, semantic states) paired with high-contrast text.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- Styled with crisp 1px borders using `neutral-300`, filling with `primary-500` upon selection. Icons must always accompany negative status indicators; emojis are strictly prohibited.
